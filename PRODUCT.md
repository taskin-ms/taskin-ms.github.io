# Taskin Mehereen: research fieldbook

Register: brand, with a reading-focused resource library.

The audience is researchers, engineers, collaborators, and people entering hypersonic compressible turbulence. The site's purpose is to build a recognizable research identity by contributing useful, attributed material. It must not claim expertise, publications, affiliations, or simulation results that have not been supplied and verified.

The homepage is deliberately spare. The primary deliverable is /hct/: a growing resource library with topic filters, search, reading paths, and a reusable reading-note format. Resources from other authors remain visibly attributed. Starter annotations describe relevance and limitations, not a claim that Taskin has personally reviewed each paper.

Voice: precise and concise. Name the method, observable, conditions, and limitations. Remove motivational slogans, metaphors, repeated caveats, and generic claims. Her commitment to participation and representation is expressed through open access to useful material, thoughtful credit, and invitations to contribute, without demographic slogans or invented outreach activities.

Visual intent: feminine, futuristic, research appropriate. Ivory and petal surfaces, deep mulberry accents, near-black text. Scientific linework and generous geometric typography supply the future-facing character. Avoid corporate blue, cyberpunk, neon, decorative telemetry, space clichés, and pink text with weak contrast. Projector readability outranks delicate decoration.

Publishing: main is the current site. The previous portfolio is preserved separately on archive/decommissioned-portfolio-2026-09; its pages and assets are excluded from the current deployment. GitHub Actions builds and checks the site before publishing. All author-editable material belongs in content/. Lead with Markdown; support MDX for component-based notes and pages. Group and sort resources automatically, index new notes automatically, and generate counts and navigation. Shared templates own HTML and visual styling. A failed content build must leave the previous website online.
