About the work

Curiosity.
Then the equations.

I’m Taskin Mehereen. My focus is hypersonic compressible turbulence, and I’m building a research practice around high-speed flows, their underlying physics, and the computational tools used to study them.

This fieldbook is part of that process: making the foundations easier to navigate, keeping assumptions visible, and turning reading into questions that can be tested.

Good questions can come from anyone. Useful knowledge should be easier to find, easier to examine, and easier to build on.
